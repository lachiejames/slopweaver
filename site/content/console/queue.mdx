# Queue

Everything waiting on your word, in one card: the steps only your hands can take, every run parked on you
with its recorded question, the replies the door is holding, the messages a session composed under your own
name and parked, the reviews of a colleague's pull request a session parked under your name as draft line
comments, and the pull requests whose next act `github landing` handed to you. Each row opens a panel that draws
the platform the thing lives on — the floating note, the board row as the terminal prints it, the pull request as
GitHub draws it — with the verb's own reason it is in the queue beside it, and a footer carrying the acts
a `slopweaver` verb already takes. Deep-link a row with `?open=<kind>:<id>`. A step that asks you to decide
opens the ask holding its choice, so its card shows that ask's problem, options, trade-offs and stakes, and its
panel the pull requests, the thread and any Slack draft it would post, exactly as it would post; a link to the
ask opens that step.

A row is its question and nothing piled before it: no kind, no action badge, no pill and no placement tag — every
label earns its place, and those are what the question already says. Its age, its currency mark and a
chevron sit on the right. An opened card carries one properties section, Linear-style — status, priority, the
workstream by its name (hover for its progress, click for its page), who the message tags and who is in its thread,
and what it is related to ([properties](/docs/console/queue-card-properties)) — and no tag pills under it. A person
is a chip only there; everywhere else on the card a person is a first name, and you are "you". Numbers in one
sentence read one way: 1.7K beside 5.9K, or 1,734 beside 5,977, never one of each — and a quoted span is someone's
words, so it is never rewritten and never decides.

## What each register is derived from

| Register       | Verb                                                         | The rows it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Lane             |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| hands          | `slopweaver hand list --json`                                | every open hand step, oldest first, the order the register publishes                                                                                                                                                                               | polled every 20s |
| asks           | `slopweaver handover list --json`                            | every board row carrying a `question` — a run parked on you — in board order                                                                                                                                                                       | read once        |
| replies        | `slopweaver queue list --json`                               | every withheld reply still `held` — the door drafted it and is holding it for your word                                                                                                                                                            | polled every 20s |
| as you         | `slopweaver slack draft list --json`                         | every parked as-me draft still `held` — composed under your own name, and nothing sent                                                                                                                                                             | polled every 20s |
| reviews as you | `slopweaver github review-draft list --json`                 | every parked review still `held` — a verdict, a body and line comments on a colleague's pull request, each drawn on its hunk, and nothing posted                                                                                                   | polled every 20s |
| judgment       | `slopweaver reading last --producer "github landing" --json` | rows whose `humanWait.next` is `name-the-reviewer` first (not one dispatched `draft-first-review-ask`, whose seat records the ask or a discussion opener), then rows whose dispatch verdict is `needs-owner-judgment`, off the tick's last reading | polled every 20s |

The judging word in every register is the verb's: a hand step is one `hand` recorded, an ask is a row the
board marks parked, a pull request is one `github landing` itself refused to dispatch to a session, and a parked draft is one `slack draft record`
wrote without any way to send it, and a parked review one `github review-draft record` wrote without any way to
post it. The page filters on those words and issues none.

## The one bar above the cards

The page opens on one bar and then the cards. **The view selector** names the view you are on and its count, and
opens a list of views, each with its count: Everything, Urgent (`priority` p0 or p1), Blocking a colleague, Blocking
the fleet, Slack drafts, Reviews under your name, New findings and Check-ins. The view rides the address as
`?view=<name>`, so a link opens on it. A tag a link carries (`?tag=key:value`) is a removable chip beside it. **The
freshness line** on the right is quiet when every card is current — _updated 2m ago_, the oldest reading on screen —
is a spinner while a read is in flight, and is a staleness mark when a register's cards are an earlier reading or
could not be read: pointing at it names every such register on a line of its own, with the verb's reason and the age
of what is still drawn. A reading that fails keeps the cards it already drew, so a poll that stumbles never blanks
the list; a press's own re-read never brings a settled card back.

The page ends at its last card, with nothing drawn under it. There is no text box: the console's one text input is the call's chat,
so answer aloud or there, and the open card is what "this" means — so an ask's panel has no act bar and no line at
its foot saying so. Where a panel's act bar explains a command rather than pressing one, the explanation sits behind
one closed line.

## A card you already spoke to leaves the list

Words you say on a call while a card YOU opened is open land on that card (`slopweaver talk cards`, polled every
20s): an ask gets them as your answer, and any other card a row carrying them, so the card waits on a session and
is not drawn again. Until that reading arrives nothing is hidden, and the gap is named as _your spoken steers_; a
card put back with `talk restore` is drawn again. An ask asking a question you already ruled on, word for word,
is drawn as a check-in carrying that ruling, never as a fresh question.

A card the voice put in front of you is different: its address carries `by=voice`, and nothing you say over it is
landed on it, so the voice reading a card out never takes it off your Queue. A steer the voice relays still lands
on it, and clicking the card yourself makes it yours, so what you say next lands as before. A relay that named no
card lands too, once the chair marks that turn as relayed: `talk cards` reads its intake mark back to the card the
voice had open while you spoke, and shows it as _recorded by the chair_. A turn the chair only answered — you asking,
not steering — lands nothing.

Moving on is reacting too. When the voice puts the next card up, by name or in a triage pass, the one before leaves
your Queue until it has news, unless you said to come back to it or parked it. That holds when you followed the card's
own link first, so no card was open as you ruled and moved on: the card the voice last put in front of you is still the
one you left, and a ruling said from its linked page can name it.

Your words land only on the card as it stood when you said them. An ask whose question is asked again after you spoke
is a new question and is drawn again; a held post whose hold or change of meaning was recorded after you spoke is
drawn again so you read why your yes is not sent. A not-a-yes hold (the reason `DRAFT_HOLD_REASONS` lists first) is not that: it is a session reading your steer
as "not yet", so the card stays off your list while the better version is made — unless its meaning changed after you
spoke.

**Recut in flight.** A card your words landed on whose work row a session is running now — in flight, its run active
— is drawn as _Recut in flight, &lt;row&gt;_ among the check-ins, off the badge, instead of the old question. Once
nothing is running it, it leaves the list until the recut comes back to you.

The card you have OPEN leaves the list but stays open until you close it or open another, its buttons held. A
plain line at its head says it has left your queue, and that once you close it you may not be able to reopen it
yet. Its **Since you opened this** section says who took it off and when, quoting the words that did it and the row a session
acts on: your words heard with the card open, your words relayed by the voice, or a session landing them from another
card — the first one that took it off, and any later ones behind a fold — and keeps naming them after a session
settles the row. A card no register holds and no reading names keeps its register's own word, and says that unless
your own press did it, no ledger this page reads names who removed it. The voice reads the same line, so it
does not ask you to rule again on a card your words already took off.

## What the badge counts, and how fresh each row is

The card has three sections, each headed with its count, in the one order every surface of what waits on you
shares. **Blocking a colleague** leads: a held reply, a post as you, a review as you, a hand step or pull request on
the work side, and an ask a colleague's address places there — a pull request, a Slack, Linear or Vanta link, never
a stated workspace alone. An ask wears the mark of the workspace it is placed in. **Blocking the fleet** is the fleet's own work stopped on your word. Those two are the number on
the sidebar badge. **Check-ins — your word is a review** come last and are never in the badge: a hand step posted
`--check-in` (a review of something already built), an ask that looks answered already, and a hand step whose ask you
already ruled on. A line in the fold at the
bottom says which context the count is taken in, how many rows name neither context (shown, and counted, in both) and how
many the other context holds and this one does not. That last figure is also a note above the list, and under the
empty state, so an empty Queue in one context never hides the cards waiting in the other; the voice says it too.

**One order, the same everywhere.** The order this list puts things in is the order the voice raises them and the
order a triage pass walks them: the order the voice raises things in matches the order the Queue puts them to you in. It opens on three sections ahead of the ones above:

- **Blocking your priorities**: any card blocking one of your priorities, by the priority it blocks.
- **Your priorities**: every card carrying a priority's board tag, your first priority first, then by its board band,
  then by the controls it resolves, most first.
- **Stopped automations**: an automation that stopped, or that you held. It is not a blocker, so it comes after your
  priorities.

A priority is a workstream on your outcomes register at priority _urgent_ that names the board tag its cards carry
(`queueWorkstream`, for ISO `iso-certification`); its rank orders it against the others. A blocker or a stopped
automation shows in both contexts, whichever one it belongs to, and putting it off does not move it down. Its decision
and options are on its face, so the choice is above the fold. The supervisor's chat update raises it in the same place
(`slopweaver queue stopping` prints those cards). None of these sections folds, so card N here is the voice's Nth.

The order is driven by the register, so lowering a priority puts the order back. It never moves a card's tier, so the
badge and the counts do not change. A check-in is never lifted. A card you put off joins no priority, and a card whose
tags read only in part never does either. When the register has never been read, a mark says so and every card stands
by its tier; when a later read fails, the priorities it last read stay lifted, marked as an earlier reading, as any
register's kept reading is.

Whether something the fleet already did was the right call is never asked here: those acts are on the
[Alignment page](/docs/console/alignment), the sidebar entry under this one, whose badge is a plain count of the
cards waiting on your answer, and nothing there blocks any work.

A collapsed row shows its title, what kind of request it is (an ask, a held reply, a draft as you, your hands…), its
priority when there is a read on one, and a **currency mark** saying when it was last checked — never when it first
reached your screen. The mark (`cardCurrencyOf`, `site/lib/cardCurrencyMark.ts`) has two halves, and a card
is **fresh** only when both were rechecked: its **facts** and **your steers**. Both come from the latest recorded
`handover currency` reading, which carries one row for every hand step, ask, held reply, draft, parked review
and pull request waiting on you, joined to its card by the card's exact key and the instant it reached you — a card
that changed since the reading is a card the reading has not checked, so a card is ranked by what needs your attention most right now, with the facts and the steers both checked. Its facts are re-confirmed by the
register still holding it, by a monitor's own last pass still naming it (never by the register alone: a monitor
gone clear leaves its card unconfirmed), by the landing poll for a pull request, or by an ask's still-real check or
a read of its Slack thread. Your steers pass only when nothing you said after it reached you could bear on it; a draft
as you counts from when its words were last written, its newest voice revision or your pick, so the words you said to
rework it are already folded in:

- **fresh · checked 3m ago** — both halves passed; the age is the older of the two. Ready to react to on first look.
  Each section lists its fresh cards first, whatever kind each is: a fresh ask is drawn above a stale hand step, never
  below it because hands are drawn first. Each kind still shows its first eight in a section, its fold straight after the last of them
  (`queueLayout`, `site/lib/queueLayout.ts`), and the voice's `action_items` ranks by the same reading, so the card it
  names first is the card numbered 1 here.
- **facts checked 3m ago · not your steers** — its facts were re-read, but you have said things since that are filed
  to it or not filed anywhere yet, one of your memories, your owner profile or a ruling of yours changed since it
  reached you, a source would not read, or the reading has not checked this card as it stands. A ruling filed to
  another row does not count unless it places a gate still in force, read from every run journal whether or not
  its doc is still on the board; a check older than schema 3 never read your
  standing guidance, so it never passes this half.
- **your steers checked 3m ago · not its facts** — nothing you said and no change to your standing guidance since
  bears on it, but nothing re-confirmed its facts, or its done-check could not run.
- **not re-confirmed · you said things since** — a check ran over it and neither half passed, so the badge names what
  each half needs rather than an age: its facts _not re-confirmed_, _facts read failed_, _facts check not run_ or _facts
  not checked_; your steers _you said things since_, _your guidance changed since_, _some steers unread_, _standing
  guidance not checked_ or _steers not checked_. An age there read as a late check when the check was seconds old.
  **not checked yet** when no check has run.
- A supervisor chat whose transcript will not read on this machine leaves a card's steers unread only when it held
  the chair after that card reached you; one that handed the chair on before then cannot hold a word about it
  (`chairSince`, `src/handover/currency.ts`).
- A read inside the check that failed — a place your words are read from, whether you replied, or what re-confirms its
  facts — is a staleness mark beside the badge and the Checked line, its hover naming what and why, never a sentence
  in the lines. The facts line says why they are not confirmed only in one of the phrases the check itself writes
  (`NOT_CONFIRMED_WHYS`, `src/handover/currency.ts`); any other reason a reading carries, which can name an id or a
  path, goes to that mark and the line says only that they are not confirmed.
- **looks answered** — you replied in its Slack thread after the message it is anchored to.
- **you ruled on it** — a hand step whose ask already carries your ruling; a seat executes it.
- **you reacted** — something you said after it reached you is filed to this card; a seat folds it in, and nothing
  on it waits on you until it comes back. A word filed nowhere yet fails the steers half but is not a reaction.

**Opening the Queue refreshes that reading**, so opening a page triggers a freshness refresh of its
cards. The supervisor tick records one whenever it runs; when you open the page and the check it shows is ten
minutes old or more, or there is none, or the latest read of it failed, the page takes its own check once
(`slopweaver handover currency --json --record-reading sync-now`) and the marks redraw when it lands. One attempt per
opening: a check under ten minutes old is kept, a run that fails leaves the reading you were shown on screen with its
real age, and a page left open asks nothing more — no timer runs behind it (`currencyOpeningRefreshPlan`,
`site/lib/currencyOpeningRefresh.ts`).

A card that is not fresh STAYS, drawn apart from the fresh ones: it may be superseded, stale or closed with nothing
needed from you. A looks-answered, ruled or reacted card moves to the check-ins, off the badge, until the chair
settles it, marks its step done, or it comes back changed. The opened card's **Checked** section is one closed line at its foot — _Checked · facts checked 3m
ago · not your steers_ — and nothing else until you open it (`CheckedFold`). Opened, it and the hover say
each half, against what, and what done means for this card — at most four lines however much you have said since, a
cap the mark's type holds (`CheckedLines`), with ages rather than instants and no transcript pointer. What you said
since sits under those lines, **folded shut behind its count** ("148 things you said since it was asked — none yet tied to
a card"; `SaidSince`, `site/components/console/CurrencySaid.tsx`), newest first and split into what is tied to this
card and what is not yet tied to any. Each row says where and how long ago you said it, then the opening
of your own words; opening the row shows the whole of them, and says when the record kept only part. A read that
failed is the staleness mark beside the line, which says only that it failed. How the check was taken is never on
the card, since no page carries a note on how it is derived: it reads the supervisor chat, your
console calls and the voice relays, and a card whose done-check is declared rather than a Slack reply says so
without listing the checks, which can name a path.

A held reply and a draft under your own name also carry the first 140 characters of the message itself, tags named
the way the card names them (`reviewLineFor`, `site/lib/queueRows.ts`) — the two kinds whose release sends
something. Every other row sends nothing when you act on it, so it has no such line.

## Every open card leads with your own words

Above its what, an expanded card says where it came from, with a direct quote of your exact words, prominent on the expanded card. An ask anchored on something you said to the
console, on a call or in a Claude session shows those words on one line as the record captured them — never a prompt
Claude Code suggested and you only accepted, nor a session's words under a call anchor naming no segment, nor a
session turn Claude Code's own wrapper was cut from the middle of — with where and
the instant that record carries — for a console turn, when it was relayed — and marked cut short wherever the record
holds more than it shows; a hand step shows the words of
the ask it belongs to, and says it is not read when that row is no longer on the live board or two rows share its
name. A draft parked for a board row, and your own pull request landing for one, show that row's words the same way,
joined as the card is drawn, so a card queued before the join shows them too; when that row should carry your words
and does not, the card says so and still draws the draft.

A card about a pull request carries **every row of yours that names it**, not only its own: the board joins rows by
the exact `owner/repo#number` each names (`prSteersByRow`, `src/handover/prSteers.ts`), before any filter — a word of
yours travels only when it is about that pull request: its row names no other, or the words themselves name it — and the
opened card draws your words stored on the other rows under one closed fold — _2 more things you said about its pull
request, on other cards_ — each placed like any quote and naming the pull request. When one of them asked for proof (a
screenshot, an Actions run link, a Datadog link) and the pull request's live body still lacks it, the card **leads
with that gap**, above its question: the collapsed row says what is missing on which pull request, and the opened card
says when you asked, in your words. The body is read by `handover currency`, the tick's or the Queue opening's, its one GitHub
read (`pullRequests` in the reading, `src/handover/prProof.ts`); a body it could not read leads with nothing here, and
the voice says it was not checked, never that the proof is missing (`site/lib/prProof.ts`).

A card no particular steer of yours produced — a fleet finding, a bot reply held for someone else, a row a session
registered with no anchor, an act, a card naming no row — quotes the **standing ask** it rests on instead, under
_your standing ask_, placed like any quote and followed by _no particular steer of yours is behind this card beyond
it_ and where it did come from. Your standing asks are recorded with
`slopweaver profile standing add <id> --anchor <anchor> --covers <coverage>`, the words read off the anchor and never
typed; each covers `findings`, `bot-replies`,
`registered-rows`, `unlinked-cards` or one exact `act:<kind>`, an act's own kind first, and one ask at most covers
each (`site/lib/standingAskSource.ts`). When none covers the card, it still says _no words of yours behind this_ or
that nothing records whose words are behind it — then that no standing ask of yours covers it; the commands a session
runs to tie the card to your words or record a standing ask fold shut under _what a session can do about it_, each
marked a session step by the code that wrote it (`site/lib/sessionSteps.ts`); while your standing asks are unread, or do not read, it says that instead. The words
are stored on the row by `slopweaver handover register`, by the card landing a call's words close, and backfilled by
`slopweaver handover quote --apply`, and a card whose anchor
names your words but carries none is refused rather than drawn without them (`site/lib/queueCardSource.ts`), and so
held off the Queue (below).

Words too long to draw whole fold shut, and the line the fold shows is the stretch of your turn that names what the
card is about — never whatever you said first, which may be filler or an aside. It is chosen by the words it shares with the card's row and by phrasing that states what
you want, with a call's fillers, stutters and pauses dropped and a muted ellipsis wherever more of your words stand
around it; it never adds a word or changes one, never starts on the card's own text read back, an `if` it cuts off,
or a stretch you took back, and a typed turn loses only its annotations. Opening the fold — _your whole words,
verbatim_ — shows every word as the record holds it (`site/lib/quoteLead.ts`).

Where you steered the same thing more than once, the rest follow it, shut under one line — _2 more times you
steered this, oldest first_ — each quoted and placed exactly like the first, so the card shows how your view moved
without growing by a list you did not open. A steer whose words would not read is said, not dropped. Last comes _why this is in front of you_:
the one line the session that put the card there wrote, drawn after your words because it is drawn from them. Words
you say on a call with an ask's card open are related to it this way, and `slopweaver handover quote <slug>
--add-steer <anchor> --apply` relates any other.

## An ask names its pull requests inline

A pull request an ask names reads as its number in the heading and the what, and the number is the link: hover
or focus it for the pull request and its diff. The URL the question carries is folded into the
number rather than printed after it, and there is no row of chips under the card. A pull request the row names
that the card never does, the Slack thread and where it was asked are lines in the folded record. In the list,
where the whole row is already a link, the number is printed alone.

## A card about a Slack thread shows the thread

When an ask is about a Slack thread — its anchor or its words name one — the card opens on the thread itself, the
way you would stumble on it in Slack: each message's author, time and text in Slack's order, read from the corpus,
with _open in Slack_ beside it. It comes before the what, rather than a sentence describing it. When a draft is held for that thread it sits under it read-only, with the
`slopweaver queue release <id>` line to copy; nothing on the card sends, and a change to its words is said in the
call's chat with the card open.

## A draft card leads with its receipts

A draft parked for your yes says first what it brings for its claim, since no card reaches you without its receipts: `Receipts: 2 links, 1 attachment.`, or why none is needed, or what was tried
and what stopped it. A draft with none of those is refused before it is parked, so it never reaches you. When Codex's
draft beside it drops the link the original brings, the card says so and choosing it means a re-cut with the link
first. A yes you gave on a draft stays given: a session cannot set it down as not a yes unless you said something
newer on that card.

## One held draft, one card

A held draft is on ONE card, since a second card for it is a duplicate of the one already approved.
When an ask or hand step asks you to send a held draft, that card owns it and the draft's own post card is not drawn:
the ask carries the problem, the stakes and your ruling, and answering it sends the draft. A link to the post
(`?open=post:<id>`, or a dropped cut's id) opens the owning card, which is team-facing as the post was. While Codex's
draft sits beside it, the owning card draws both — labelled by who wrote each, since the ask's letters are its own
options — and an ask's what opens on the choice: _Codex drafted the same message blind to Claude's draft … say which
one goes before either can be sent._ An ask naming several held drafts owns them all but takes no post card, since the
voice reads one draft or one pair per card, so each keeps its own. Any further card naming a draft another card already owns is held off the Queue
(below), naming the draft and the card that owns it (`site/lib/queueDraftOwnership.ts`). A sent or dropped draft is a
record, not a decision, so any number of cards may say what became of it.

## The acts, and what each one does

| Panel      | Button                             | Verb                                                                                      | What it writes                                                             |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| your hands | Mark done                          | `slopweaver hand done <id> --did "done, marked on the console" --by-session work-console` | a `settled` row on the hand register; the note comes down; the row leaves  |
| your hands | Not now: 1 hour, 3 hours, Tomorrow | `slopweaver hand snooze <id> --for 1h\|3h\|tomorrow --by-session work-console`            | a snooze record and marker — the step stays OPEN, the note comes back then |
| your hands | Set (a secret field)               | `slopweaver secrets set <name> --stdin --json`, the value on stdin, never the argv        | the secret, `0600` in the store; the field reads `set <name>`, no value    |

Every control prints the exact words it records, and every one is a click: the console has no hotkeys. The panel is
folded: its question and where to check it sit above the fold, and the whole briefing — what happened, why it is
yours, the stakes, the options and where it was read from — is one disclosure away. A press **settles** the row
(`components/console/settling.tsx`): the panel closes at once, the row leaves the queue and every count, and
one line above the list says what was recorded, with what the verb wrote behind _what the verb wrote_. The row comes
back only if the verb refused or its outcome is unknown — then the line says so and the register's re-read decides.

A hand step's panel is not a briefing: it draws the step's own fields — what it unblocks, where to go, what to
paste, what settles it, what it cites and who posted it — and a field the step left off is no row. A step opening
a shaped ask adds that ask's own problem, options, lean and stakes. A card that would draw a sentence neither
record wrote is refused, and held off the Queue rather than drawn.

## A card that fails its own checks never reaches you

Every card passes the same checks before it is drawn: no sentence its record did not write, your words present when
its anchor names them, a draft it asks about that it can actually show, no draft drawn twice, no held draft another
card already owns, a page it cites cited
first, and no session, call, talk, turn or run named by its machine id. A card that fails one is **held off the
Queue**: out of every list, count, badge, triage walk, spoken item and recently-left card, and its words are never
drawn, so a card that reaches you always passed every requirement and the Queue fails closed. One
line above the list says how many are held, a link to one opens a note saying it is held and which check held it, and
the supervisor's tick reads every held card with `slopweaver queue refusals --json` and sends a session to fix the
record that same pass. An act the fleet took is not held: those are the Alignment page's, and a finding act reads as
refused until its pull request is looked up (`site/lib/queueWithholding.ts`).

**A reason the Queue quotes is said plainly, never pasted in.** Where the Queue's own sentence rests on a read that
failed — a stored quote of yours that does not read, your standing asks, a pull request a finding act looks up, a
detached run's doc, a word of yours on its pull request — the sentence is the Queue's alone, the read's reason sits
beneath it in plain words, and the raw message is closed under **Full message**. Held
cards' checks, refused cards and the source block all draw it the same way (`site/components/console/ReasonLines.tsx`),
the same shape a staleness or warning mark already has; the voice and `queue refusals --json` read it as one line.

The words are also refused where they are written: `slack draft record` refuses a `--why` or `--meaning-changed`,
and `slack draft hold` a `--why`, that names a machine id, and `hand post` refuses a step whose card would draw one
in any prose field — its sentence, `--witness`, `--unblocks`, `--if-ignored`, `--no-address`, a checklist line or a
carried decision — so the session fixes them while it still holds them.

A recorded ask has no button: `handover answer` is gated on the run's writer session, which this page is
not, so the answer is given aloud or in the call's chat, as the top of this page says. A parked as-me draft
has none either, and that one is the point rather than a gap: releasing it posts under your own name, so the
panel prints `slopweaver slack post --as me --draft <id> --approve <digest>` behind its one closed line for you to run — `slack reply` for a
draft that replies in a thread, whose card names the message it goes under — and offers no press. A pull request has no enqueue
button yet, for the reason on [the console overview](/docs/console#what-is-not-on-it-yet).

A press is one POST to `/api/act/<slug>`, the argv is built from `site/lib/actVerbs.ts`'s declared slots and
nothing else, and the answer is one of four: **done** with what the verb wrote, **refused** with the verb's
own reason, **unknown** when the verb was cut off on its timeout — the register is re-read either way, so the
list and the panel move together. Reproduce any of them yourself:

```bash
slopweaver hand list --json
slopweaver handover list --json
slopweaver queue list --json
slopweaver slack draft list --json
slopweaver reading last --producer "github landing" --json
```

## What is not here, and why

The supervisor chair's open-pages index — the pages it has put in front of you with their options — is a
scratch file the chair carries from one seat to the next, not a document any `slopweaver` verb prints. Every
field on this site comes from a verb a reader can run, so that list waits for a verb that prints it rather
than a route that reads a chair's scratch directory. Email drafts and enqueue are absent for the reasons the
page itself states beneath the card.
